# Academic biography

I am an International Baccalaureate student studying in Hong Kong, with a particular interest in finance, economics and the growing influence of technology on financial markets. I want to understand how financial decisions are made, why markets behave as they do and how those decisions affect businesses and wider society.

My interest has developed through studying economics and mathematics, following current affairs and writing independently about questions that interest me. This portfolio brings together some of that work and shows how my understanding has developed through research, analysis and writing.
